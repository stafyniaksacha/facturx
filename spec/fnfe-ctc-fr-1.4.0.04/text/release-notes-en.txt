Preamble
In accordance with Standard XP Z12-012, the documentation for the technical application components required to
perform the format and profile validations specified in this standard must be completed.
The first step is to produce the invoice validation schematrons (Flux 2) for the two data syntaxes (UBL and UN/CEFACT
CII, which is also the syntax used for Factur-X).
This is the subject of this publication by the FNFE-MPE, which sought to produce common components intended to
serve as a reference—and even to be used by as many parties as possible—in order to ensure standardized validation
processing.
This publication concerns only the schematrons for validating France-specific business rules described in Standard
Z12-012, with the exception of those corresponding to multi-vendor invoices (BR-FR-MV) and those for B2G rules (BR-
FR-CPRO), which will be published in a later version. It also contains the rules applicable to the CDAR message
(Lifecycle Statuses).
The contributors to these components cannot under any circumstances be held liable for any direct or indirect
damages, loss of use, loss of profits, loss of data, loss of communication, loss of revenue, loss of contracts, or operational
losses that may result from their use.
It is therefore up to each user to conduct their own tests and to submit comments and any identified bugs to the FNFE-
MPE (currently atschematronReformeFE@fnfe-mpe.org ; a GitHub repository is under construction and will be
available during the summer of 2026) in order to contribute to the continuous improvement of these products.
To facilitate use, the Schematron components for standard controls defined in the EN 16931 standard, as well as
certain XSDs, are also included, in the versions current as of the date of this publication. They are also available from
the entities that publish and update them.

IMPORTANT - MAJOR CHANGE to the EXTENDED-CTC-FR profile (implemented in
version 1.3, completed in version 1.4)
The EN 16931 standard allowed only one reason for VAT exemption per invoice and did not permit invoices containing
VAT categories “out of scope” (O) alongside other categories (standard, exemption, reverse charge, etc.). This has been
corrected with the revision of the standard, which will require specifying the reasons for exemption, discounts, and
charges at the document level, and then taking them into account in the VAT breakdown validation rules (BG-23).
At the same time, the ability to use exemption reasons for VAT categories “S” and “Z” was also added.
Since this is one of the major changes—as it adds data that must be entered online or in the return or at the document
level at a minimum whenever VAT categories other than “S” (standard) or “Z” (0% VAT, not to be confused with
Exemption, Reverse Charge, Export, …), this capability has been added to the EXTENDED-CTC-FR (and EXTENDED)
profiles.
However, to allow for a transition period, the VAT breakdown check is performed BOTH WITH and WITHOUT taking
these VAT exemption reasons into account—whether entered online, submitted, or uploaded at the document level.
The invoice is considered compliant as long as one of the two checks passes. However, if either check fails, this is
indicated in “warning” mode, specifying whether it relates to the “ini” rule (WITHOUT grounds) or the “rev” rule (WITH
grounds).
Finally, since this change applies to multi-vendor invoices, it must be followed for these invoices.
For the EXTENDED-CTC-FR profile or the EXTENDED profile of Factur-X, this change—which requires specifying
exemption reasons using codes and/or text in the “online” field and in the “discounts and charges” section at the
document level for any VAT category other than “S” or “Z,” consistent with the VAT breakdown—will become
mandatory as of July 2026.
For all invoices except multi-vendor invoices, this simply involves entering these reasons in the “online” and
“document-level credits and debits” fields in the same manner as the information provided in the VAT footer.

1 Reminder on the Organization of Checks
Invoice validations are performed through the following sequence of checks:
• Step 1: A structural validation of the invoice or credit memo message using an XSD:
✓ For UBL: the UBL 2.1 XSD, available at https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.1/. In UBL, there is one
XSD for invoice-type messages (UBL-Invoice-2.1.xsd) and another for credit note-type messages (UBL-
CreditNote-2.1.xsd).
✓ For CII: the UN/CEFACT D22B XSD, attached (CrossIndustryInvoice_100pD22B.xsd: a single, identical
message for invoices, corrected invoices, and credit notes). The “uncoupled” version—that is, without
validation of the code lists embedded by UN/CEFACT—SALL BE USED since the EN 16931 standard
deviates slightly from it regarding codes for charge reasons or discounts. These codes will be validated in
the next step. For EXTENDED profiles, a dedicated Schematron will be required to allow for the addition of
certain data and the modification of certain business rules.
✓ For Factur-X, an XSD has been published for each profile to reduce it to the minimum useful structure. Only
the XSDs for the three selected profiles—BASIC WL (Factur-X_BASICWL.xsd), EN16931 (Factur-
X_EN16931.xsd), and EXTENDED (Factur-X_EXTENDED.xsd) are included in versions 1.09 and 1.09.2Beta
for the EXTENDED profile (in anticipation of a 1.09.2 patch to version 1.09 dated June 10
th
, 2026).
• Step 2: Validation of the business rules for the EN16931 standard and the EXTENDED-CTC-FR profile:
✓ These Schematrons validate:
▪ Reducing the XSD structure of each syntax to the precise scope of the implementation of the EN 16931
standard (reducing branches and leaves of the syntactic model), as well as to the EXTENDED-CTC-FR
profile, respectively.
▪ The specific business rules, whether regarding the reduction of certain cardinalities, calculations, tax
rules, conditional rules, the number of decimal places, or compliance with code lists
✓ Each Schematron is therefore linked to a syntax and a profile as follows:
▪ For the EN16931 profile: the schematrons of the EN16931 Standard (in UBL and CII), published and
updated every 6 months by the European Commission, are attached here in the version applicable as of
May 15
th
, 2026 (and also available at THIS LINK). It should be noted that these Schematrons specify
“warning” validations rather than “fatal” errors for data that is present in the invoice but not in the
EN16931 semantic model, which means these invoices are not rejected.
▪ For the EXTENDED-CTC-FR profile: a dedicated Schematron, published with this version 1.4.0 (file
names do not include version numbers to facilitate deployment from one version to the next):
EXTENDED-CTC-FR-UBL.sch and EXTENDED-CTC-FR-CII-.sch.
▪ For Factur-X: each profile with its own XSD has a corresponding Schematron. Since the Factur-X
EXTENDED profile is broader than the EXTENDED-CTC profile (of which it is a subset), it can be used
for the CII’s EXTENDED-CTC-FR profile. As a reminder, since Factur-X is based on UN/CEFACT CII, it is
possible to use Factur-X validation components for a UN/CEFACT CII invoice.
IMPORTANT: For those wishing to implement multi-vendor invoices, the EXTENDED profile must be
used. Version 1.09, released in June, requires a patch to allow multiple VAT detail lines for categories
other than S. The provided EXTENDED profile anticipates this patch.
• Step 3: Apply the additional controls specific to the reform in France, which is the subject of this
publication. To do so, simply apply the following schematrons, which have been designed to run in the
same way across the different profiles:
✓ For the version applicable to UBL (EN16931 and EXTENDED-CTC-FR profiles):
BR-FR-Flux2-Schematron-UBL.sch.
✓ Version applicable to UN/CEFACT CII (and therefore also for Factur-X profiles in the factur-x.xml file
attached to the PDF/A-3): BR-FR-Flux2-Schematron-CII.sch.
• Potentially, Step 4: for invoices intended for the public sector (B2G) and therefore for Chorus Pro: a
Schematron corresponding to the BR-France-CTC-CPRO rules, currently under development, may be used
(one UBL and another for UN/CEFACT CII).

For the lifecycle message, the principle is the same, but with only two steps:
• Step 1: Syntax validation using the “uncoupled” Cross Domain Acknowledgement and Response (CDAR) XSD,
since the code lists are specific
(CrossDomainAcknowledgementAndResponse_100pD22B.xsd).
• Step 2: Validation against French business rules for using the CDAR message for lifecycle statuses using the
Schematron published by the FNFE: BR-FR-CDV-Schematron-CDAR.sch.
All of this demonstrates that invoice and CDV validations must be organized as a sequence of syntactic checks (XSD)
based on the message’s syntax and potentially its profile, or even its invoice or credit memo type (in UBL), AND
multiple phases of business rule validation at various levels (international standards, French regulations,
business domain). There may also be a slight variation depending on the expected processing type (integrated into the
BR-FR-Flux2 Schematron through the use of the “Note” element with subject code “BAR” and content such as “B2B,”
“B2BINT,” “B2C,” etc.):
• Domestic B2B subject to the electronic invoicing requirement: flow 2.
• Outbound international B2B: flow 8.
• International B2B purchase transactions: flow 8, but issued from abroad and therefore not necessarily subject
to specific French rules.
• Or even B2C: flow 9.
2 Scope of this publication and work plan
2.1 Scope of this publication
This publication consists of 3 Schematrons in accordance with the specific control management rules described in the
XP Z12-012 standard, version 1.4.0 dated June 30, 2026:
• BR-FR-Flux2-Schematron-CII.sch, and its compiled version (XSLT), applicable to invoices in the UN/CEFACT
CII D22B syntax (including factur-x).
• BR-FR-Flux2-Schematron-UBL.sch and its compiled version (XSLT), applicable to invoices in UBL 2.1 syntax,
for the INVOICE message and the CREDITNOTE message (to be used for credit memos).
• BR-FR-CDV-Schematron-CDAR.sch and its compiled version (XSLT), applicable to Life Cycle messages in CDAR
(Cross Domain Acknowledgement and Response D22B) syntax, for invoice statuses (between Accredited
Platforms or destined for the PPF).
The invoice Schematrons apply equally to the various profiles (EN16931 and EXTENDED-CTC-FR, as well as BASIC
WL, EN16931, and EXTENDED from Factur-x).
These are Schematron validators for invoices or lifecycle messages; therefore, they do not implement the mapping
rules (BR-FR-MAPXXX), which are intended to correct the extraction of data from flows 2 or 8 in order to construct
flows 1 or 10.1, to account for specific PPF requirements regarding certain invoice data.
This version of the Schematron does not implement the validations related to the additional ChorusPro management
rules (BR-FR-CPRO-01 through BR-FR-CPRO-50). They are therefore limited to the following rules:
• For invoices (flows 2, 8, 9) issued by a VAT-registered entity established in France:
✓ business rules BR-FR-01 through BR-FR-32,
✓ the conditional business rules BR-FR-CO-01 through BR-FR-CO-17,
✓ the rules for managing the number of decimal places BR-FR-DEC-01 through BR-FR-DEC-04,
✓ the business rules for multi-vendor and bidirectional invoices BR-FR-MV-01 through BR-FR-MV-14, and
BR-FR-BD-02 and BR-FR-BD-13.
• For lifecycle messages:
✓ the management rules BR-FR-CDV-01 through BR-FR-CDV-16,

2.2 Comments
The published version has undergone extensive testing but is subject to corrections and optimization.
To this end, these components are published on GitHub (https://github.com/fnfempe/France_RFE), where they are
publicly available to facilitate feedback and suggestions for improvement.

2.3 Work Plan
This work is intended to be supplemented by the following elements:
• An additional Schematron dedicated to B2G rules (BR-FR-CPRO).
• Updates to the Schematrons following the incorporation of most changes from the revision of the EN 16931
standard into the EXTENDED / EXTENDED-CTC-FR profile (in advance of the implementation of this revision
before 2029), and transitioning to the new UBL 2.6 and UN/CEFACT CII D25A versions.
• Schematrons for extracting Flow 1 or Flow 10.1 components from Flow 2 or Flow 8, taking into account the
mapping management rules described in the XP Z12-012 Standard.
• A version optimized for processing time is also in development.
If necessary, work may continue on components for converting between the minimum framework formats (described
in the XP Z12-012 standard), or even on creating standard LISIBLE files for both the EN16931 and EXTENDED-CTC-
FR profiles.
